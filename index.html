FAQs

HVAC Questions From Mount Brydges Homeowners

The things neighbours in Mount Brydges, Strathroy, and the surrounding country ask us most.

How quickly can you reach Mount Brydges for a no-heat call?

We are based right in Mount Brydges, so most no-heat calls in the village are same-day, and we reach Strathroy, Glencoe, Delaware, Appin, and Melbourne quickly as well. Propane and gas furnaces are a short drive for us, not a trek across the city.

We heat with propane out in the country. Do you work on those systems?

All the time. A lot of rural properties around Mount Brydges run propane furnaces, and our technicians service them regularly, from ignition and pilot issues to full propane-to-heat-pump conversions when you are ready to stop scheduling deliveries.

Can you install a mini-split in my farm workshop or outbuilding?

Yes. Mini-splits are one of our most common jobs out toward Appin and Melbourne. We size the unit to the building, run the line sets cleanly, and mount the outdoor unit level on a pad that drains so it lasts.

Are smart thermostats worth it on a propane furnace?

Yes, when they are wired and programmed correctly. On propane especially, a proper setback schedule trims fuel use over a long winter. We match the thermostat to your furnace, heat pump, or multi-stage setup so the schedule actually holds.

Do you clean the ductwork in older Mount Brydges homes?

We do. Many village homes here have ductwork that has collected decades of dust, pet hair, and construction debris. We seal and pressurize each vent so the dust goes into our vacuum, not back into your rooms, and your furnace and AC stay protected throughout.

Which communities around Mount Brydges do you cover?

Mount Brydges itself, plus Strathroy, Glencoe, Delaware, Appin, and Melbourne, along with the rural farm properties in between. If you are in Middlesex County and unsure whether you fall in our area, call us at (548) 761-2072.
