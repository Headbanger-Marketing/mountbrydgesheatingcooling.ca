About Us

Mount Brydges Heating & Cooling, Local HVAC for Middlesex County Homes and Farms

We are the local crew your Mount Brydges neighbours call when the propane furnace quits or the AC gives out in the humidity. Licensed, insured, and based right here in Middlesex County, we keep village homes and surrounding farm properties comfortable through every season.

Our Story

Small-Village Care, Whole-System Expertise

Mount Brydges Heating & Cooling started with a simple goal: bring honest, dependable HVAC service to a corner of Middlesex County that big-city companies treat as a detour. We know the century homes in the village that run on natural gas, the rural properties that depend on propane deliveries, and the growing number of neighbours switching to cold-climate heat pumps.

What started as furnace and AC repair has grown into mini-splits for farm workshops, propane-to-heat-pump conversions on rural properties, smart thermostat setups, and duct cleaning for the older village homes that need it most. The work has changed over the years, but the approach has not: diagnose the real problem, explain it plainly, and fix it without the upsell.

Living and working in Middlesex County shapes how we do the job. A short drive to Strathroy, Glencoe, Delaware, Appin, or Melbourne means faster response times when something quits, and we treat every home, workshop, and farmhouse like it belongs to someone we will see at the local store next week, because most of the time it does.

What We Work On

Propane and natural gas furnaces, central air and heat pumps, cold-climate mini-splits for workshops and outbuildings, smart thermostats, gas fireplaces, and the aging ductwork that comes with older Middlesex County homes. Residential and light farm properties, all handled by one local crew.

Our Commitment

How We Approach the Work

We believe in fixing the real problem, not the most profitable one. If a capacitor brings your AC back to life, that is what we replace, and if your propane furnace has years left in it, we say so. Honest diagnoses and upfront pricing are the baseline on every visit, not an upgrade.

That honesty shows up in the details too. Technicians who show up on time, protect your floors, clean up when they finish, and take the time to explain what they found. It is the way we would want our own homes treated, and it is why neighbours across Mount Brydges and the surrounding country keep our number on the fridge.
